MCP Guides for Short-Form Video

Two ready-to-use short-form video hooks, visual scripts, and execution guidelines for promoting TrueProfit MCP.

Video Short-Form MCP Promotion Script Guides

Why Promote TrueProfit MCP?

TrueProfit MCP connects a merchant's live profit data directly to AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT, so they can ask questions about profit, costs, and performance without opening dashboards or manually copying numbers.

  • Instant answers: Merchants can ask about profit, costs, and performance in plain language.
  • Live store context: Once connected, the AI can understand their numbers without repeated setup.
  • Root-cause insights: MCP helps explain why profit changes by looking across COGS, ad spend, fees, refunds, and more.
  • Works with AI tools they already use: Claude and ChatGPT become easier ways to explore store performance.

✦Learn more about TrueProfit MCP›

How to Promote TrueProfit MCP in Short-Form Video

Short-form video is perfect for showing, not just telling. The "AI already knows my store data" moment is highly visual and surprising. Focus your videos on the workflow of asking an ecommerce question and getting an instant, context-aware answer.

  • Use screen recordings of the AI chat answering a specific store question.
  • Hook viewers with a relatable pain point (e.g. manual spreadsheets, dashboard fatigue).
  • Keep the tone casual and helpful, like you're sharing a workflow hack.
  • Don't explain how the integration works technically'just show the result.

Key Notes for Short-Form Video

  • Keep videos under 60 seconds (30-45s is ideal).
  • Text overlays are crucial for retention.
  • Ensure the screen recordings are readable on mobile devices.

Final Thoughts

Short-form video thrives on visual proof. By demonstrating exactly how much faster and smarter the MCP workflow is, you can convert viewers who are tired of manual profit tracking and spreadsheet math.
