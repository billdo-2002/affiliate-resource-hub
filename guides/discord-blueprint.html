How to Set Things Up

To get started, you'll need an active E-commerce or Dropshipping community - whether on Discord, Skool, Whop, or another platform.

Next, choose a natural spot in your community to mention TrueProfit and share your affiliate link, such as:

  • A dedicated Discord channel
  • A course lesson or module
  • A pinned community post

Pro tip:

Explain why accurate profit tracking matters before introducing TrueProfit. Use a simple example, pain point, or real workflow to make the recommendation more credible, then keep your affiliate link and any discount easy to find once the value is clear.

Dos and Don'ts for Communities

What to Do What to Avoid
Keep discussions focused on helping sellers understand their real cash flow. Do not spam your tracking link in general discussion channels or direct messages.
Use personal experiences and actual P&L struggles to build authority. Do not write robotic, corporate messages that sound like official support copy.
Recommend the tool as a way to automate and save time. Do not pitch the app as a “get rich quick” utility. Keep it grounded.

Why featuring TrueProfit inside communities works

People join e-com communities for solutions. When you feature TrueProfit to help members automatically aggregate costs and track real net margins, it comes across as a genuine community benefit rather than a promo. Because your members already trust your guidance, sharing TrueProfit turns that established credibility into effortless side income.

Key Takeaways

  • E-commerce communities exist to solve problems; position TrueProfit as the natural solution to spreadsheet fatigue.
  • A dedicated channel for profit-related discussions ensures your links stay contextual.
  • Keep your tone friendly, direct, and focused on helping other sellers scale safely.
